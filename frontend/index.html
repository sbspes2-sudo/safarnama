    About this site

    Front end only, honestly labelled

    What this is

    A design concept built in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No backend, no accounts, no payments and no real bookings - the fares and hotel rates are real numbers found online, but each is a dated example from a named source, not a live quote.

    What a real launch needs

    A flight and hotel search API, live availability, payments, and a server. Until then, treat every price here as "seen online on 30 Oct 2026" and re-check before planning.
